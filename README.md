# Folio

A minimal, smooth notes app for the Mac: notes, checklists and folders.

## Install

1. Download **[Folio.dmg](https://github.com/maniomix/folio-releases/releases/latest/download/Folio.dmg)**.
2. Open it and drag **Folio** into **Applications**.
3. Open Folio. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because Folio isn't
   signed with a paid Apple developer account. Click **Done**, then open
   **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to
   Folio.

## Updates

Folio checks for new versions every hour and installs them by itself the next time you
quit it. You can also choose **Folio › Check for Updates…**.

Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 or later.
